# Triggers

Read these slowly, one area at a time. Each word is a hook, not a question to answer: capture whatever it pulls up and keep going.

## Work
- Manager, team, colleagues, clients, partners
- Promises you made; things others are waiting on from you
- Things you're waiting on from others
- Meetings coming up: prep, follow-ups, notes not yet sent
- Projects to start, finish, unblock; problems nobody has solved
- Decisions you keep postponing
- Reports, reviews, approvals, deadlines
- Email, chats, documents, tickets you meant to answer
- Tools, accounts, access, admin, expenses
- Hiring, feedback, one-on-ones
- Career: skills, courses, the next step

## Home
- Repairs, broken things, things that annoy you every day
- Cleaning, clutter, things to sell or give away
- Purchases: groceries, household, gifts, replacements
- Garden, car, bike, pets
- Moving, renovating, furniture

## Money
- Bills, subscriptions to cancel, refunds
- Taxes, receipts, savings, investments
- Loans, debts, money lent to someone
- Insurance

## Paperwork
- Passports, IDs, visas, licences expiring
- Contracts, warranties, registrations
- Letters to answer, forms to fill

## Health and body
- Doctor, dentist, check-ups, tests
- Prescriptions, medicines, glasses
- Exercise, food, sleep
- That thing you've been ignoring

## People
- Family, partner, kids, parents
- Friends you haven't called; messages owed
- Birthdays, anniversaries, gifts
- Favours promised or asked
- Conversations you've been avoiding

## Digital
- Files, photos, backups, passwords
- Phone, laptop, updates, storage
- Inbox and notification clutter

## Time ahead
- Trips, holidays, events, tickets
- The next few weekends
- Season changes: clothes, tyres, school year

## Growth and fun
- Books, films, courses you meant to start
- Hobbies, sports, instruments
- Places you want to visit
- Things you'd like to try someday

## The rest
- Worries and "what ifs"
- Ideas that keep coming back (these go to the ideas box later)
- Anything you told yourself "I'll remember"
